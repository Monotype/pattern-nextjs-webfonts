Place your licensed .woff2 files in this directory (same folder as this file).

- Obtain files under a valid Monotype web font license for self-hosted delivery.
- Font binaries are gitignored — do not commit them to this repository.
- The sample in app/page.tsx uses Gotham_normal_normal_350_subset1.woff2. Change
  the `src` path in the localFont() call to match your filename.
