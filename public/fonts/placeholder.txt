Place your licensed .woff2 files in this directory (same folder as this file).

- Obtain files under a valid Monotype web font license for self-hosted delivery.
- Font binaries are gitignored — do not commit them to this repository.
- The sample in app/page.tsx expects a file named MyFont.woff2. The existing
  MyFont.woff2 is a heavily subsetted version of Gotham Regular for demo purposes.
  Either copy over it with a font of your choice in your local workspace
  or change the `src` path in the localFont() call to match your filename.
